# The Road to Sharm el-Sheikh (COP-27)

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*zEXawhLA0jsCeBR5fODG_g.png)

Kohei Noda, Ph. D
HR consultant, mental counsellor and cognitive scientist in field. Originally spending carrer in Japan as HR consultant pursuing studying human depression. After 2011 of big earthquake, Tsunami and nuclear reactors explosion in Fukushima, moved to the Philippines. Spent 8 years there for SDGs/development education in Manila, Cebu and Dumaguete in the Philippines. After COVID-19, came back to Japan and started CCL-Japan, CPA(Climate Pyschology Alliance) Japan, CCA (Climate Coaching Alliance) Japan, Zero Emission Yokohama. Also board member of JANIDA (Japan NGO for International Development in Africa).

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*nNbVhQMImpU6Borecq7-aw.png)

### I did not have much awareness for climate change

Until early 2020, when my business stopped for ESL/SDGs training school in the Philippines and a HR consultancy job in Japan due to COVID, I hadn’t spent much time or thought about climate change/emergency. Originally, my work centered on mental health and how people can live happily, so I have been working on the SDGs (/MDGs) and the three disruptions advocated by Pachamama Alliance or Presencing Institute. For environmental and social disruption, I could honestly sympathize with the idea of ​​“personal”, but I think the most distant was the field of climate change. In 2018, when I got together with my Japanese colleagues to translate the Drawdown website, my interest and involvement was so low that I helped a little with the translation.

### COVID has changed my attention to climate change

However, in 2020, everything stopped due to COVID, and I was strongly encouraged to take time to think about the current situation of the earth and humankind again in Theory U community, and to take action in the new program (“pathway to reverse global warming”) by Pachamama Alliance (PA). Maisa from PA guided me at several coaching sessions during her busy schedule.

With the additional help of my Pachamama colleagues in Japan, I contacted the Citizens’ Climate Lobby (CCL), which appears in the climate change module of Game Changer Intensive (GCI) course of PA. And CCL staff especially the international staff strongly recommended me to launch a local CCL there because Japan is one of G7 countries but did not have a branch even it is already an international environmental NGO with 600 branches and 200,000 volunteers over 60 countries. Then I was guided to emerge CCL Japan with the help of Theory U community in Japan.

### Even after starting activities, I still felt it was not enough

CCL’s main target policy worldwide is “carbon fee and dividend” in accordance with climate justice. During our two years of activity, I strongly feel that the awareness of citizens is not sufficiently spiked compared to the other western developed countries which have responsibility to lead climate change action from the legacy of emitting GHG a lot in past.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/1024/1*YAdEq-VVKUVcMqcjrIXXrw.png)

I returned to Japan at the end of November 2020 after 8 years stay in the Philippines and in September 2021, I started another local initiative Zero Emission Yokohama in my city. I also registered CCL Japan to Climate Action Network Japan which is famous for the “fossil of the day award” in COP. I also brought Climate Psychology Alliance into Japan and working for Climate Coaching Alliance in Japan. I’m doing my best as an individual, but I still feel it is not enough and need more awareness.

### What could be done more?

Personally I studied physics and nuclear engineering in college so I dont have difficulty to go into the field of climate science. And in addition luckily I have management consulting background so I am okay to talk about national policy making or whatever. That is why I try to use my capacity best to do some effort climate change.

Now shifting my interest from personal happiness, societal development to the basis of our society which is planet earth helped by the moment of pause at COVID-19.

FFF (Fridays For Future) youth are spending their time and worrying their long future in their life. Then why not spending our time more as a responsible adult?

### COP27 can be used for raising awareness?

In United Nations, every year they have a conference discussing current climate change action by the countries joined to the treaty called UNFCCC (United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change) since 1992 (Earth Summit timing).

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/281/1*wpBkceqkLm4BKcSiRy51tg.png)

*[https://cop27.eg/](https://cop27.eg/)*

Following the risen awareness of climate change, in last COP26 in UK, about 30k participants were in the conference. From Japan about 250 deligates were there in UK but only few (like about 10) civil society members (from NGOs) were there checking all the list from Japan.
(I excluded foreign nationals coming to UK using Japanese org.’s name etc.)

Looking at the other countries, many civil society members are gathering in the place and report a lot.
Youth (FFF like Greta Thurnberg) are there.
From Japan, like last year at least two youth are planning to get there and startd fundraising for that already.

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/614/1*GcuQO6SL9kVssy6mJPmziQ.png)

Again, why not as a responsible adult get there, observe, report and share?
