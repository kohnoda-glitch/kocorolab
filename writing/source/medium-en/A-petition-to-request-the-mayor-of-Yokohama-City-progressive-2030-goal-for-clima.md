# A petition to request the mayor of Yokohama City progressive 2030 goal for climate crisis

I am a member of the group for realizing zero-emission Yokohama.
(I am a lead of Citizens’ Climate Lobby Japan as well.
[https://citizensclimate.earth/
](https://citizensclimate.earth/)[https://japan.citizensclimatelobby.org/](https://japan.citizensclimatelobby.org/))

The city I live in Japan is one of the two C40 cities in Japan.
([https://www.c40.org/](https://www.c40.org/))

Following the change of NDC from 26% by 2030 to 46% by 2030, our city is changing our goal of GHG emission reduction by 2030 this year.
Ahead of that, in last month in Feburary, our mayor Takeharu Yamanaka commented to aim 50% GHG emission decrease from 2013 to 2030.

The city has not opened the official draft of the plan for global warming countermeasure for 3 to 4 years from 2022 yet. The draft will be shown to citizens and public comments are accepted as well as submitted to local congress and discussed.

Now, Climate Action Tracker announced that Japan needs to reduce their GHG more than 60% by 2030 from 2013.
[https://climateactiontracker.org/press/japans-paris-agreement-target-should-be-more-than-60-by-2030_analysis/](https://climateactiontracker.org/press/japans-paris-agreement-target-should-be-more-than-60-by-2030_analysis/)

We had a research and a seminer asking to AIST(The National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology) researcher Dr. Manabu Utagawa ([https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Manabu-Utagawa-2](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Manabu-Utagawa-2)) if Yokohama can reduce more than 60% by 2030 and the simulation result was “possible.”

So before the city open thier plan soon this year, we started requesting petition from all over the world to show the mayor that we wish city raise their local NDC to at least 60%.

The change.org petition is here.
[https://www.change.org/p/%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%AD%E7%AB%B9%E6%98%A5%E6%A8%AA%E6%B5%9C%E5%B8%82%E9%95%B7-%E6%A8%AA%E6%B5%9C%E3%81%8B%E3%82%89%E7%A7%81%E3%81%9F%E3%81%A1%E3%81%A8%E6%AC%A1%E3%81%AE%E4%B8%96%E4%BB%A3%E3%81%AE%E6%9C%AA%E6%9D%A5%E3%82%92%E5%AE%88%E3%82%8B%E3%81%9F%E3%82%81%E3%81%AE%E6%B8%A9%E6%9A%96%E5%8C%96%E5%AF%BE%E7%AD%96%E5%AE%9F%E8%A1%8C%E8%A8%88%E7%94%BB%E3%82%92](https://www.change.org/p/%E5%B1%B1%E4%B8%AD%E7%AB%B9%E6%98%A5%E6%A8%AA%E6%B5%9C%E5%B8%82%E9%95%B7-%E6%A8%AA%E6%B5%9C%E3%81%8B%E3%82%89%E7%A7%81%E3%81%9F%E3%81%A1%E3%81%A8%E6%AC%A1%E3%81%AE%E4%B8%96%E4%BB%A3%E3%81%AE%E6%9C%AA%E6%9D%A5%E3%82%92%E5%AE%88%E3%82%8B%E3%81%9F%E3%82%81%E3%81%AE%E6%B8%A9%E6%9A%96%E5%8C%96%E5%AF%BE%E7%AD%96%E5%AE%9F%E8%A1%8C%E8%A8%88%E7%94%BB%E3%82%92)

### A global warming countermeasure implementation plan to protect us and the future of the next generation from Yokohama!

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/0*HcB2lCusQ_3rnePn)

[**Yokohama**](https://www.change.org/o/%E3%82%BC%E3%83%AD%E3%82%A8%E3%83%9F%E3%83%83%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%B3%E3%82%92%E5%AE%9F%E7%8F%BE%E3%81%99%E3%82%8B%E4%BC%9A%E6%A8%AA%E6%B5%9C)** started this petition to Takeharu Yamanaka Mayor of Yokohama**

A major cause of global warming is carbon dioxide (CO2) emitted by burning fossil fuels.

When the temperature of the earth **rises by 1.5 degrees Celsius** from the Industrial Revolution …

・ Heavy rain and flood damage more frequently

・ Typhoons are becoming more and more intense

・ Summer is longer and hotter, and the number of deaths from heat stroke increases significantly.

・ The impact on food security such as agricultural products and fisheries is becoming more serious.

・ Seawater temperature rises and 90% of corals die

・ The sea level rises, and the number of people who lose their place of residence and are affected by floods and storm surges is increasing.

And now, the temperature of our earth has already **risen by 1.2 degrees Celsius** .

Now, more boldly, national and local governments need to implement **policies to avoid the climate crisis .**

The city of Yokohama, which is a 3.7 million city, is currently revising the “ **Global Warming Countermeasures Implementation Plan .”**

In the plan, I want you to set an ambitious CO2 reduction target and lead Japan’s global warming countermeasures!

Therefore, I will submit the signature to the Mayor of Yokohama.

Please support us.

Signature:

Raise the 2030 greenhouse gas reduction target for Yokohama City

The Government of Japan has raised its greenhouse gas reduction target to 46% (compared to 2013) and will continue to take on the challenge of reaching a height of 50%. At the “26th Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (COP26)”, it was agreed to limit the average temperature rise of the earth to 1.5 ° C, and it was decided to strengthen and review the targets of each country.

Considering the carbon budget to achieve the 1.5 ° C target, the 2030 reduction target needs to be 60% or more (Note).

For these reasons, it is necessary to accelerate the reduction of greenhouse gases by 2030.

Please raise the 2030 greenhouse gas reduction target of Yokohama City to 60% or more and lead the creation of a carbon-free society in Japan.

(Note) The international research institute Climate Action Tracker points out that Japan should set the reduction target for 2030 to 62% or more. According to estimates by Manabu Utagawa of the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology, 60–65% by 2030 by thoroughly improving energy efficiency such as heat insulation and establishing a system to shift to renewable energy in Yokohama City. Can be reduced.
