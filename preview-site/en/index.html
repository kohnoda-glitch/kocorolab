FOUNDER

Founder

Kohei Noda, Ph.D., is President of Kocoro Laboratory, Globis University, Research Faculty, and regional faculty for MIT Sloan IDEAS Asia Pacific.

He served as head of human resources at both a pre-IPO growth company and listed operating companies.
