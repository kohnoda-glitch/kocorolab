Founder

Kohei Noda, Ph.D., is President of Kocoro Laboratory, Associate Professor at GLOBIS University, and Regional Faculty for MIT Sloan IDEAS Asia Pacific.

He served as head of human resources at both a pre-IPO growth company and listed operating companies: Gulliver, INTLOOP, and Change. This site does not discuss individual cases or people from those roles.
